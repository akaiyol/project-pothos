# Figma Version 1 material refinement

2026-10-03. Owner-authorized static mockup revision in [Figma](https://www.figma.com/design/XAcSMlGq99YjRtf7In3Xna/Untitled?node-id=0-1). No production behavior or complex UX work.

## Confirmed scope

- Replace the blue/pink background with cool grey glass, slight blue tone, shine and white highlights. Keep the treatment simple, clean and cyber.
- Replace the repetitive diary-cell palette with varied, randomly distributed shades similar to the original history graph. Synthetic colours represent sample daily selections, never contribution intensity.
- Preserve the existing composition, colour wheel and copy for this focused pass. Their presence does not approve unresolved UX or mood labels.
- Do not add stars, motion, navigation, writing or saving controls in this pass. These remain part of the broader direction, outside this revision.

## Visual specification and acceptance

- One rounded glass surface with a neutral cool centre, subtle blue edge and restrained reflective highlights; no pink tint.
- Retain the top seven-row calendar, month alignment, cell geometry and heading. Use muted gold, blue, peach and mauve families with different shade depths and an irregular distribution.
- Keep diary colour fills opaque so glass cannot alter their represented colours.
- Inspect static compositions at 320, 390 and 430 points against light and dark surroundings. This does not verify interactions, native accessibility or responsive product behavior.
- Text and graph remain unobscured; shine stays subordinate to content.

## Data, states and limits

Existing populated synthetic grid only; no data storage, transmission or permissions. Empty/loading/error/save and accessible interaction states remain outside this static material study. Complex UX is explicitly deferred by the owner. Preserve the broader briefs for subsequent work.
