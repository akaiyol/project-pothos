# Project Pothos — Seasonal Ecosystem

Updated: 2026-10-03. Status: owner-directed concept; platform and release boundary unresolved. No production implementation is authorized.

## Product direction

Project Pothos is moving away from a traditional diary toward a personal ecosystem shaped by daily colour contributions and emotional patterns. Seasons, glassmorphism, and the deliberate clash of nature with cyber materials define the direction. The name and intended meaning, a longing to feel whole, are retained.

## Confirmed direction

- Screen one retains daily colour selection, contribution history, and space for thoughts and feelings.
- Selector candidates are a rim colour wheel, a two-dimensional colour field, and a gradient between two colours. No candidate is selected.
- Workflow: choose a colour → record it in history → represent that contribution as one cluster of leaves in the ecosystem. The persistence boundary remains unresolved.
- Screen two is a cyber-nature tree. Its leaves and flowers use colours from saved contributions.
- Spring, summer, autumn, and winter each have their own tree section and branches. Spring includes leaves and flowers; the other seasons use distinct foliage whose forms remain to be designed.
- Sentiment patterns over time bring creatures to life in the ecosystem.
- Bugs represent simple emotions; animals represent complex emotions. These are authored symbolic associations, not objective psychological categories.
- A visitor catalogue records the bugs and animals that have visited.
- A shiny variant has a requested 5% chance of a different colour. The probability event and persistence rules remain unresolved.

## Proposed details

- An ordinary text box feels too simple; a richer thoughts interaction will be ideated later. No replacement is approved.
- A sentiment graph is desired, but its dimensions, data source, placement, and relationship to colour remain unresolved.
- Dark, obsidian-like glass for trunk and branches is a candidate cyber treatment. The broader cyber visual language is open.
- Five generally happy days causing something to come alive is an illustrative pattern, not an approved threshold.
- Candidate associations: ladybug—happy-go-lucky; butterfly—sadness; rabbit—turmoil; hedgehog—uncertainty. The complete mapping needs review.

## Product boundaries

User-selected colour remains authoritative for history and foliage. Colour alone does not establish sentiment. The source and interpretation of emotional evidence must be agreed before an algorithm is specified. Missing entries are missing information, not negative emotions. Creatures should not grade a person's emotional state.

Privacy, accessible non-colour descriptions, editing/deletion effects, sparse-data behaviour, and uncertain interpretation must be defined before implementation. No cloud processing, clinical interpretation, platform, or AI model is selected.

## Superseded direction

The traditional notebook as the mandatory shell, sealed seasonal artwork/reveal, scrapbook as the core destination, photograph-resonance matching, and stars-only diary MVP are removed from the active plan. Existing studies remain historical evidence; this update does not alter their visual output.
