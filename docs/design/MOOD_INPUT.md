# Colour Input and Sentiment

Updated: 2026-10-03.

## Colour selection

Compare a rim colour wheel, a two-dimensional colour field, and a two-colour gradient. No form, axis semantics, or emotional endpoint labels are approved. Record the user's selected colour faithfully in history and leaf clusters. Whether the gradient is a selection surface or a stored pair of colours remains open.

## Sentiment direction

The owner wants a sentiment graph and pattern-driven ecosystem events beyond sentiment-to-colour mapping. Neither mapping has an approved algorithm. User-selected colour must not be overwritten by inferred sentiment.

Before specifying the system, resolve:

- Evidence source: optional writing, explicit emotional input, user-defined colour associations, or a reviewed combination.
- Representation: simple polarity cannot by itself establish complex emotions such as turmoil or uncertainty.
- Time windows, missing days, mixed emotions, confidence, and minimum evidence.
- User review/correction and whether sentiment inference is optional.
- Graph dimensions and placement; pattern-to-visitor rules and explanations.
- Effects of entry edits/deletion and processing failure.

Five generally happy days is a candidate scenario, not a rule. No evidence should produce no inference, rather than a fabricated mood. Use synthetic records for future design evaluation; no model or backend is selected.
