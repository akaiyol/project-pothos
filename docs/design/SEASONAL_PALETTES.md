# Seasonal Colour and Foliage

Updated: 2026-10-03.

Saved contribution colours supply tree leaves and flowers. Spring, summer, autumn, and winter have separate sections and branches; spring includes leaves and flowers. The remaining seasons need distinct foliage designs, including the winter form.

Earlier seasonal palettes remain [reference studies](archive/2026-10-03/SEASONAL_PALETTES.md), not a requirement to restrict daily choices. Final palettes, selector mapping, hemisphere/season boundaries, year rollover, and exact gradient representation remain unresolved. Season appearance must not replace or reinterpret the user's recorded colour.
