# Glass Material — Ecosystem Direction

Updated: 2026-10-03. Glassmorphism remains central; the traditional glass diary and stars-only MVP are superseded as the product direction.

The owner proposes dark, obsidian-like glass for the cyber-nature tree's trunk and branches. This is a material candidate, not an approved render. Leaves, flowers, and creatures carry the nature component; the full cyber language remains unresolved.

Earlier grey/blue glass, white highlights, reflection and contrast studies may inform later material work. Their existing screenshots verify only those earlier studies, not this tree concept. Motion, sensors, decorative stars, and additional effects are not automatically carried into the new scope.
